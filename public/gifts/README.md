# First Gift & Bracelet Video Assets

## 1. First Gift Image (`public/gifts/`)
- Place the image of your first gift (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp`, or `.gif`) inside **`public/gifts/`** (for example: `public/gifts/first-gift.png`).
- Open [`src/data/configuration.ts`](../../src/data/configuration.ts) and update `SITE_CONFIG.giftBoxExperience.firstGift.imagePath` to `'/gifts/first-gift.png'`.

## 2. Bracelet 3D Video (`public/videos/bracelet-reveal.mp4` & `src/assets/videos/bracelet-reveal.mp4`)
- Your 6-second 3D bracelet reveal video (`BBB.mp4`) is already installed at **`public/videos/bracelet-reveal.mp4`** and **`src/assets/videos/bracelet-reveal.mp4`**.
